# Show digital product presentations to doctors.

> **Route:** `/app/e-detailing`

## In Short

Show digital product presentations to doctors.

## Real Example

Amit shows SYNHEART slide deck on tablet to Dr. Verma.

## What Data Is Here?

- PresentationId
- ProductId
- Slides/Documents
- ViewLog

## Workflow

1. Upload presentation
2. MR opens in field
3. Shows to doctor
4. View time logged in DCR
