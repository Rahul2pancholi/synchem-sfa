# Update product prices in bulk.

> **Route:** `/app/Product Price Updation`

## In Short

Update product prices in bulk.

## Real Example

MRP of SYNPAR changed from ₹30 to ₹32 — update all.

## What Data Is Here?

- ProductId
- OldPrice
- NewPrice
- EffectiveDate

## Workflow

1. Select products
2. Enter new price
3. Apply
